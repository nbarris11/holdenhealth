# Holden Health SEO launch checklist

The code-level SEO foundation is complete. These external steps require the business owner and should be completed when the production domain goes live.

## Before connecting the domain

- Confirm that `https://www.holden.health/` will be the primary domain. The site currently redirects the non-`www` version to it.
- Confirm the published prices: Fitness $199/month, Nutrition $199/month, and combined coaching $299/month.
- Replace branded image holds with compressed WebP or AVIF photography. Use descriptive alt text that describes the actual photo; do not stuff location keywords into every image.
- Create a real 1200 × 630 social-sharing image and replace the current logo-only `og:image` values.
- Review all credentials and service claims with Kelsey one final time.

## Google setup

1. Create or claim the Holden Health Google Business Profile.
2. Use the real public location if clients can visit it. Otherwise configure Holden Health honestly as a service-area business; do not publish a home address solely for SEO.
3. Keep the business name, phone, website, categories, hours, and service area consistent everywhere they appear.
4. Add the main category that most accurately reflects the business and add relevant services individually.
5. Verify a Google Search Console Domain property for `holden.health`.
6. Submit `https://www.holden.health/sitemap.xml`.
7. Inspect and request indexing for the homepage, Coaching, Online Personal Training, Online Nutrition Coaching, and Meet Kelsey pages.
8. Run the homepage and two service pages through Google's Rich Results Test after deployment.

## Other discovery channels

- Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
- Update Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile, and any legitimate professional directory profiles to use the final website URL.
- Ask real clients for honest Google reviews. Never write, purchase, gate, or mark up invented reviews.
- Pursue useful local mentions: Eastern Michigan alumni features, Metro Detroit community organizations, partner studios, wellness professionals, and local media. Relevance matters more than bulk directory links.

## First 90 days

- Publish one genuinely useful article every two to four weeks based on questions clients actually ask.
- Prioritize topics that support the two service pages: beginner strength training, short workouts, protein habits, eating out, and getting back on track after a missed week.
- Link each article naturally to the most relevant service page.
- Monitor Search Console queries, impressions, click-through rate, indexing, and page experience monthly.
- Improve pages that earn impressions but few clicks before creating more pages.

## Important

No ethical SEO work can guarantee first place. The site is technically prepared to compete, but local rankings also depend on proximity, a verified Google Business Profile, real reviews, useful content, trustworthy mentions, and time.
