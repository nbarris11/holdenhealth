More than the workout

Someone will ask how your week really went.

Nutrition and habit coaching are part of every session, with weekly habits you track in your member portal and direct accountability with Kelsey. The goal is not a perfect report card. It is noticing what worked, what didn't, and what support would help next.

Common questions

What to know before your first class.

Focus on You is built to feel clear and welcoming, especially if group fitness has felt intimidating before.

Do I need group fitness experience?

No. Kelsey demonstrates the movements and offers options for different starting points. Her beginner's guide to strength training explains what a comfortable first workout can look like.

What should I bring?

Bring water and wear clothes and shoes you can move comfortably in. Dumbbells, bands, medicine balls, stability balls, and other training equipment are provided.

Where are classes held?

Classes meet at Moore Performance Health, 41100 Plymouth Road, Suite B1-162, in Plymouth, Michigan.

What if I miss my usual day?

Class plans and makeup options depend on the session. See the selected program’s dates and attendance policy before joining.

Is nutrition coaching included?

Yes. The session includes practical nutrition and habit support through habit tracking and direct accountability. It is general coaching, not medical nutrition therapy.

Who will coach the classes?

Kelsey Holden has coached since 2011 and holds personal training, group fitness, nutrition coaching, and Exercise Science credentials.

Would one-to-one training suit me better?

Choose a group if you enjoy shared class times and training alongside other people. For an appointment focused on your individual goals, explore personal training with Kelsey in Plymouth. You can talk through both options in a free consultation.

Before you join

Payment, attendance & cancellations

Choose how to pay

After you talk with Kelsey, she’ll send you a sign-up link that shows the session’s price. Pay online through Stripe, or reserve a place and pay by Venmo, cash, or check before your first class.

Make the schedule work for you

For a weekly plan, tell Kelsey if you need to switch class times or make up a missed class within the same session. An all-classes package covers the scheduled classes in its dates. Missed classes do not carry into a future session.

If your plans change

Cancel before the program starts and you will receive your money back. Once the session begins, missed classes are not refundable. If a serious illness or injury prevents you from participating, contact Kelsey to discuss your situation.

If Kelsey must cancel

If Kelsey needs to cancel a class because of an emergency, she will provide an at-home workout instead.

Joining after the start?

There are no drop-ins currently. Contact Kelsey about joining a session already in progress. If there is space, she will confirm a prorated price.
