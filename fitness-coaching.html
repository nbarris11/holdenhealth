How your coaching week works

Your workouts are built around the goals, equipment, fitness level, and schedule you discuss with Kelsey. Follow your assigned plan at home or in your own gym, and record your workouts in the Holden Health app.

Use your weekly check-in and one-to-one Zoom conversation to review progress, ask questions, and explain what made the week easier or harder. Kelsey uses that feedback to adjust your plan. Between check-ins, you can send her a private message in the app.

If your available time changes, tell Kelsey so your plan can change with it. The exact exercises and training days are personal to you; there is no single sample calendar every client must follow.

Weekly Zoom coaching is a check-in with your coach. It does not mean every workout is a live, supervised video session. If you prefer a coach beside you while you train, consider in-person personal training in Plymouth.

Common questions

Online personal training, plainly explained.

Do I need a gym?

No. Kelsey can build your workouts around a full gym, dumbbells, resistance bands, or a simple home setup. See her 20-minute full-body strength workout for an example of how a short plan can still be complete.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Exercises, volume, and progression are chosen for your current ability. You do not need to get in shape before starting. Kelsey's beginner's guide to lifting explains what a comfortable first strength workout can look like.

What happens during the weekly Zoom session?

You and Kelsey review your progress, talk through challenges, answer questions, and make sure the next week still fits your goals and life.

What if my routine keeps falling apart?

The plan includes modifications and practical backup options. Kelsey adjusts the work instead of treating a busy week like failure.

Is coaching available outside Metro Detroit?

Yes. The program is delivered online, so clients can participate throughout the United States.

How is this different from using a workout app?

Some apps give you a workout library or automatically generated plan. Holden Health includes an individual coaching relationship with Kelsey: personalized programming, weekly conversations, and adjustments based on your feedback. Our online personal trainer vs. workout app guide explains what to compare before choosing.

What happens after the four weeks?

Discuss your next steps and ongoing coaching arrangements with Kelsey. Start with the consultation so you understand the current package and how payment works before committing.
