# Holden Health Focus on You Session Hub

## Revised recommendation

Build for the business Kelsey has now: in-person, group-based total-body conditioning sessions delivered in short cohorts.

Use **Holden Health: Focus on You** as the working program name. Present each cohort as an edition beneath it, such as **Focus on You: 6-Week Group Fitness & Nutrition Session** or **Focus on You: December Mini Session**. This keeps the idea recognizable when the duration changes while tying a widely used phrase to the more distinctive Holden Health brand. A preliminary web search found “Focus on You” used by other fitness businesses and many competitors as general marketing language, so it should not be treated as a unique or legally cleared standalone brand name.

Do not build individualized workout programming, exercise logging, subscription billing, progress charts, wearables, messaging, or a large exercise-video library yet. Kelsey does not currently use those workflows, and building them now would mean guessing how her future business should operate.

The first portal should make joining and completing a six-week session feel organized, personal, and professional. It should also give Kelsey a simple roster and content-management tool.

## Current customer journey

1. A visitor finds Holden Health online.
2. The visitor emails or submits an interest form.
3. Kelsey connects with the visitor by phone or email.
4. Kelsey confirms whether the six-week session is a good fit.
5. The client chooses the two- or three-class-per-week commitment and pays through Venmo, Zelle, or check before the session begins.
6. Kelsey marks the enrollment as paid and sends an account invitation.
7. The member signs in and sees everything needed for the six weeks.

This should be the workflow the first portal supports.

### Recommended customer-facing attendance language

> Choose the two- or three-day option that fits your goals. Classes are offered Tuesday at 6:00 a.m., Wednesday at 7:00 p.m., and Saturday at 9:00 a.m. Your days can change when life changes—just attend another available class during the same session. Missed classes are not refundable and cannot be carried into a future session.

Because the full cohort is capped at 25, switching days cannot exceed the program's overall enrollment cap. A lightweight weekly attendance selector is still useful for room setup and planning, but it does not need to function as a strict reservation system.

### Recommended cancellation starting point

Do not publish “no refunds except in extreme circumstances.” It is subjective and can produce inconsistent decisions. Use a written policy that defines the timing and remedy. A practical starting point for Kelsey to approve is:

> Your registration reserves a limited place in the full program. Payments are nonrefundable once the session begins. If a serious injury or medical condition prevents participation, contact Kelsey promptly. Holden Health may, at its discretion and with appropriate documentation, provide a prorated credit toward a future session. Missed classes are not refundable, but may be made up at another available class during the same session. If Holden Health cancels a class, a replacement class or appropriate credit will be provided.

This is operational copy, not legal advice. Have the final enrollment agreement reviewed for Michigan compliance. Michigan's Attorney General specifically recommends putting cancellation and refund promises in writing and ensuring the written contract matches what customers are told.

## Kelsey's admin experience

### Session management

- Create a six-week session.
- Set start/end dates, meeting dates, time, location, capacity, price, and enrollment status.
- See attendance counts, close a full class, and move a member to another available class.
- Add week-by-week descriptions and announcements.
- Duplicate a prior session to create the next cohort.
- Mark a session as enrolling, full, active, completed, or cancelled.

### Roster and accounts

- Add a prospective participant.
- Record consultation status.
- Invite an approved member to create an account.
- See invitation, enrollment, payment, and check-in status.
- Pause, remove, or archive an account.
- Export a roster.

### Manual payment tracking

- Record method: Venmo, Zelle, or check.
- Record amount, date received, and an optional internal note.
- Mark pending, paid, refunded, or waived.
- Never store bank information, Venmo credentials, check images, or full financial-account details.

Stripe is not needed for the first release unless Kelsey decides she wants online card payments. Manual payment status is sufficient for the workflow described.

### Check-in review

- See who has or has not completed the current check-in.
- Read all four answers together.
- Write a private response.
- Mark the response as reviewed.
- See prior weekly check-ins for that member.

### Website content

Give Kelsey structured editors for the content she is most likely to change:

- Current six-week session details.
- Enrollment status and call to action.
- Homepage headline and introduction.
- About biography and credentials.
- Coaching/session descriptions.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Prices and policy summaries.
- Photos and social sharing image.
- Resources and announcements.

Do not provide unrestricted HTML editing or a drag-and-drop layout builder. Use labeled fields, image previews, draft/preview/publish controls, and version history so Kelsey cannot accidentally break the site.

## Account recommendation

Use invite-only member accounts for the first release.

Visitors should not create accounts before Kelsey confirms that the session is a good fit. After the conversation and payment commitment, Kelsey creates the enrollment and sends an email invitation. This matches her current personal sales process and prevents unused or spam accounts.

## First-version data model

- `profiles`: member name, email, phone, and account status.
- `staff_roles`: Kelsey's administrator permission.
- `sessions`: cohort name, dates, time, location, price, capacity, and status.
- `session_meetings`: individual class dates and week descriptions.
- `enrollments`: member, session, enrollment stage, and invitation state.
- `payment_records`: manual status, method, amount, date, and internal note.
- `check_ins`: four answers, week, submitted date, review state, and coach response.
- `announcements`: session-specific updates.
- `resources`: public or member-only files and links.
- `site_content`, `site_media`, and `content_versions`: editable website content.
- `audit_events`: invitations, role changes, payment-status changes, publishing, and account removal.

## Technical approach

- Next.js with TypeScript for the public site, member hub, and admin portal.
- Netlify for deployment and preview environments.
- Supabase Auth for invite-only accounts.
- Supabase Postgres for sessions, enrollments, check-ins, and content.
- Supabase Storage for public website photos and protected member resources.
- Row Level Security so members can only see their own enrollment and check-ins.
- Mandatory multi-factor authentication for Kelsey's administrator account.
- Transactional email for invitations, password recovery, check-in reminders, and announcements.

Continue serving the current static site until the replacement passes testing. Preserve every public URL and SEO field during migration.
