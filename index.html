The Wednesday Plan

Train. Nourish. Thrive.
In that order.

This isn't a catchy slogan pasted on a program. It's the order we work in: build strength, make food feel simpler, and create a routine that leaves room for the rest of your life.

  • Train. Get stronger with workouts made for your body, equipment, and actual week.
  • Nourish. Make food choices that support your goals without rigid rules or a second full-time job.
  • Thrive. Build enough confidence and flexibility to keep going when life gets messy.

The Holden Health app

Your next workout. Your daily habits. One place.

The Holden Health app keeps your workouts, habit check-ins, and progress together—with guidance from Kelsey along the way.

  • Know what’s next. Open your workout and follow the instructions at your own pace.
  • Keep small promises. Check off the habits you’re working on, one day at a time.
  • See your consistency. Look back at completed workouts and the progress you’re making.
  • Stay connected between workouts. Send Kelsey a private message or catch up with your program group, right inside the app.

Every membership includes full access to the Holden Health app at no extra cost. Your workouts, habit check-ins, progress, and member messaging, all in one place.
