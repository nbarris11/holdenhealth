Your starting point

A trainer you can ask questions.

You do not need a perfect routine before you ask for help. The consultation is a chance to explain what you want from training and what has made it difficult to get there.

  • You are new to strength training and want individual guidance.
  • You are getting back into exercise after time away.
  • You want an appointment that makes room for your own goals.
  • You prefer working directly with a coach to choosing workouts on your own.

Prefer the energy of a group? Explore Kelsey's group fitness classes in Plymouth. If travel or appointment times are the obstacle, online personal training may fit better.

Before you book

Personal training, clearly explained.

How much does personal training cost?

Each in-person session with Kelsey costs $100 and lasts 60 minutes. Start with a free consultation lasting 30–60 minutes to discuss your goals and whether the service fits.

Can I start as a beginner?

Yes. Tell Kelsey about your experience and anything you feel unsure about during the consultation. You can ask about learning movements, building a routine, and choosing an appropriate starting point.

What happens before my first workout?

Book a consultation first. You and Kelsey discuss your goals, experience, and schedule, then arrange training appointments directly. Read what to expect when starting personal training for questions to bring and directions for your first visit.

Do I need my own equipment?

Equipment is provided at the Plymouth location. Bring water and wear clothes and shoes you can move comfortably in.

Is this the same as online coaching?

No. This service is a one-to-one, in-person appointment in Plymouth. Online coaching is a separate four-week program with personalized workouts, weekly Zoom coaching, and support between check-ins.

How often should I book?

Discuss your goals, availability, and budget with Kelsey during the consultation. Agree on a schedule before booking ongoing appointments.
