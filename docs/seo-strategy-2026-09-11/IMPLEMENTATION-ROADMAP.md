# Implementation roadmap

## Weeks 1–4: equal foundations

Local: inspect GBP ownership/eligibility and real business details; improve personal-training and class landing pages; prepare review process and partner listing requests. Online: refine online service positioning and delivery explanation; add permissioned proof and buyer questions. Shared: retain indexing setup, audit lead attribution without collecting health details, improve homepage mobile loading where practical. Deliverable: two clear acquisition paths with measurable consultations. Dependency: Kelsey's verified facts and external-account access where needed.

## Weeks 5–12: expand useful coverage

Execute the alternating local/online calendar. Obtain legitimate local mentions and relevant fitness/community references. Build actual client evidence with permission. Review search queries and each path's leads separately. Maintain group-session dates and availability on an evergreen URL.

## Months 4–6: improve demonstrated opportunities

Prioritize pages with relevant impressions or consultations. Assess nearby-city demand with localized data before adding any page. For online, deepen topics that produce qualified leads rather than indiscriminately broadening to weight loss or medical specialties. Keep equal business priority while reporting differing results.

## Months 7–12: strengthen recognition

Develop original coach-led resources, client stories and partnerships. Reassess local versus online revenue, capacity and conversion. Expand only where distinct user needs and real evidence justify the work.

## Ownership and limits

Agent: research, technical implementation, draft page improvements, audits and reporting. Kelsey/user: factual programming details, client/photo permission, business-profile verification steps when required, and approval to send outreach. No fabricated links/reviews or indiscriminate directory submissions. Existing weekly heartbeat tracks launch indexing for four weeks; this document does not silently extend it into a year-long campaign.

Proposed operating effort: roughly one focused content update plus one short evidence/review session weekly, adjusted to founder availability. Monetary budget and capacity not provided. Rank and revenue targets remain unset until a reliable baseline exists; no invented authority score or guaranteed timetable.
