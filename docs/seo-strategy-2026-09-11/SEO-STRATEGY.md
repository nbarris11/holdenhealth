# Holden Health search growth strategy

September 11, 2026. User explicitly prioritizes local bookings and US online coaching equally. This is a proposed strategy, not authorization to publish new positioning or invented service facts. Allocate approximately half of planned growth work to each track; shared technical work benefits both.

## Objective

Generate qualified consultations, paid personal-training clients, class enrollments and online coaching purchases from non-brand searches. Technical eligibility and Lighthouse SEO scores do not establish competitive rankings. Five priority pages have indexing requests accepted; Search Console is still processing.

## Positioning

Recommended shared theme: personal coaching for people starting strength training or rebuilding consistency, with workouts that fit real schedules. Existing copy supports this. Do not narrow the business to women, mothers, older adults, rehabilitation or another specialty without Kelsey's confirmation.

Local: personal training and group fitness in Plymouth, Michigan. Start with the actual training location. Evaluate Canton, Northville and Livonia opportunities after confirming actual customer catchment and examining geographic search results. Do not imply facilities in other towns.

Online: personalized online strength training for beginners and adults returning to exercise, across the US. Explain the four-week plan, weekly coaching and between-session support accurately. Weekly Zoom check-ins must not be described as live supervision of every workout or promised video form review unless confirmed.

5. Improve existing guides and publish useful purchase-decision content linked to the matching service. Target buyer questions rather than generic high-volume workout traffic.

## Measurement and decision rules

Track local and online separately: qualified organic consultations, consultation-to-paid conversion and attributable revenue. Track non-brand impressions/clicks by landing page and query cluster. Use Search Console for query discovery, not invented traffic estimates. Keep sensitive intake/health information out of analytics.

First 30 days: establish analytics/booking-attribution capability and baseline; inspect indexation and query relevance. By 90 days: compare local and online non-brand visibility and qualified leads against baseline; improve pages earning impressions but weak clicks or leads. Set numerical lead/revenue targets only after Kelsey confirms capacity and measured conversion rates. At six months, expand demonstrated winners; at twelve months, reassess revenue efficiency and market coverage. Equal effort does not guarantee equal results or timing.
