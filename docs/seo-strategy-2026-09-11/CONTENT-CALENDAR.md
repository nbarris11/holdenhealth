| Week | Track | Deliverable | Conversion destination |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Local | Expand personal-training page: experience, suitability, first visit, FAQs and proof | Local consultation |
| 2 | Online | Refine online training page around beginners and consistency, with accurate delivery details | Online consultation |
| 3 | Local | What to expect at your first personal-training session in Plymouth | Personal training |
| 4 | Online | Improve existing beginner lifting guide with Kelsey's practical coaching explanation | Online training |
| 5 | Local | Small-group fitness or one-to-one training: choosing between Kelsey's actual offers | Group classes and personal training |
| 6 | Online | Online personal trainer or workout app: what support are you paying for? | Online training |
| 7 | Local | Approved local client story, or first-session FAQ update if no permissioned story is available | Relevant local offer |
| 8 | Online | Improve existing getting-back-on-track guide with a coach-reviewed busy-week example | Online training |

Photo/testimonial consent and accurate outcomes required. Avoid invented transformations, promised medical outcomes, or claiming results from demo portal data. Review existing content for overlap before adding a new URL. Titles describe planned topics, not verified search-volume opportunities.
