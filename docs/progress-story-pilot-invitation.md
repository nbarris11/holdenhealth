## Member invitation

Subject: Help us try your new Holden Health progress story

Hi [First name],

We’re trying a new part of the Holden Health portal that connects your workouts to what matters to you outside of training.

Would you take two minutes to open Progress and answer: **What would getting stronger let you do?**

It could be enjoying a hike, keeping up with your kids, feeling comfortable in a class, or making time for yourself. Use your own words—there’s no right answer.

If you’ve noticed a small win lately, you can save that too. Your goal and wins stay private between you and the coaching team. They’ll give Kelsey a starting point for your first personal recap.

**Save my goal:** https://portal.holden.health/portal/progress#story-goal

Sign in with your usual member email. If anything feels confusing, reply and tell us where you got stuck.

Holden Health

## Kelsey's first recap

Open https://portal.holden.health/admin/stories after a pilot member saves their goal. Read their goal and recorded activity, then write a short note connecting one actual observation to what matters to them. Save the draft, preview it, and publish when ready. A newly saved goal alone is not evidence of physical improvement.
