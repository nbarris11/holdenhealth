Privacy policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Holden Health LLC respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you share

When you request a call, send a message, request a resource, or join The Wednesday Note, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, goals, preferred call time, and anything you choose to include in your message.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to you, provide requested resources, send emails you chose to receive, discuss coaching services, and keep basic business records. We do not sell your personal information. You may unsubscribe from optional emails at any time.

Service providers

This website is hosted by Netlify. Form submissions may be processed and stored by Netlify so they can be delivered to Holden Health. Email and scheduling providers may also process information when we reply or arrange a call.

Retention and your choices

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations. You may unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email, or ask to review, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing HoldenHealth.Coaching@gmail.com.

Health information

Please do not submit sensitive medical information through a general website form. Coaching intake information, if needed, will be requested through an appropriate process.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website and services change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
